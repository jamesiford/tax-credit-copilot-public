# Slide outline: at most six slides

Short. The briefing holds the detail. Every figure on a slide is copied from a tool result; no
chart you compute, no total you add. A slide with a table shows the returned rows, not a
summary of them. Put the disclaimer and the provenance footer in the footer of every slide
(a short form is fine: the disclaimer sentence as returned, and the `Provenance:` line).

| Slide | Title | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `<Client>: credit review` | The client, the programme and the tax year. One line on what this deck is. |
| 2 | Position | The credit figure and headcount lines from `client_eligibility`, as written. |
| 3 | Movement | The trend sentence and, if it fits, the table from `client_trend`. |
| 4 | Jobs at risk, other programmes | The headline sentence from `client_at_risk` and the programme names from `client_discovery`. No predictions. |
| 5 | Decisions | The findings the user decided and what was recorded; the findings still open. |
| 6 | Next steps | The package link as returned, and what the consultant does next, in your words, with no figures. |

Speaker notes: one or two sentences per slide, in your words, no figures. Where a slide quotes a
figure, the note says which tool it came from.

Verify all slide text and all notes with `verify_figures` before saving.
